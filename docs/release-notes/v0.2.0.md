### Features

- **Click a shape to find its code.** Clicking a shape in the diagram selects the statement that draws it, including inside containers, and for a shape a macro made, the line that calls the macro. A drag still pans.
- **Line numbers** in the editor, counting the lines of the file.
- **Several cursors at once,** with Xcode's shortcuts: ⌃⇧-click or ⌘-click adds a cursor, ⌃⇧↑ and ⌃⇧↓ add one on the line above or below, ⌥-drag selects a column, and ⌥⌘E selects the next match. Esc goes back to one cursor.
- **Find and replace** with ⌘F while the editor has focus. Elsewhere in the browser, ⌘F is still the browser's own find.
- **Help ▸ Check for Updates…** says whether a newer version is out. On the desktop it offers the download; in the browser, a new version waits for you to click Reload.
